## Accomplishments

- Added registration regression coverage for stale-session copy and kept registration-specific backend errors visible.
- Updated `RegisterScreen` so `Sua sessao terminou. Entre novamente para continuar.` and the accented variant are not rendered in the account creation flow.
- Replaced visible create-child CTAs with `Cadastrar criança` in the responsible children screen and responsible dashboard.

- Added `Retornar às crianças` on responsible home, routed through `ResponsibleTabsScreen` to `ChildProfileSelect`.

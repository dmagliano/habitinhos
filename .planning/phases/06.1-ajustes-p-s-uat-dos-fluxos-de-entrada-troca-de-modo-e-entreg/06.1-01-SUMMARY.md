## Decisions Made

- Registration persists the JWT by default because account creation should leave the responsible user inside the newly created family session.
- Login defaults `Mantenha-me conectado` to unchecked so token restoration is explicitly opt-in.
- The auth stack now has separate typed routes for welcome, login, and register, making the post-UAT entry flow testable.
